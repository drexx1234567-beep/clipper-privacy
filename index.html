Clipper – Privacy Policy

Clipper is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload short videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and to back up the owner's own files to Google Drive.

It does not collect, sell or share any data with third parties. Google account data (YouTube uploads, channel statistics, Drive files created by the app) is used only on the owner's own computer and is never transferred elsewhere.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
